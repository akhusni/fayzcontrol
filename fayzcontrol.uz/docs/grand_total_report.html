1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

Fayz Medical House HIS & Clinical EMR is an enterprise hospital management and accounting engine engineered around three non-negotiable architectural pillars:

1. Single Source of Truth for Clinical Billing:

Every medical charge exists as a concrete, dated line item in invoice_items. Total billing is derived via $\sum \text{invoice\_items.total\_amount}$. Invariant date consistency is enforced via CONSTRAINT chk_invoice_items_dates. Cancelled/refunded stays resolve to 'refunded'.

2. Strict Separation of Concerns & Sanitation Workflows:

- Database Triggers: Confined strictly to reactive mathematical rollups, ledger synchronization, and invariant rebalancing.

- Application Service Layer (db.py): Encapsulates multi-table operational workflows (admit_patient, transfer_patient_bed, discharge_patient) inside atomic Python BEGIN ... COMMIT transactions. Mandatory clinical sanitation (beds.status = 'cleaning') transitions automatically upon patient departure.

3. Turnover Collision Resolution & CAD Projections:

In v_bed_live_status, the window order a.start_date ASC, a.created_at DESC guarantees that on turnover morning, the incumbent occupant retains the bed until formally discharged. Upcoming reservations are projected via next_reserved_date.

4. Comprehensive Schema Dictionary (21 Base Tables)

Group A: Physical Space Infrastructure

  • rooms (12 rows): Physical hospital rooms across Floors 1 and 2. Enforces capacity and functional room classifications (standard_ward, vip_ward, consultation, nurse_station, reception, utility).
  • beds (14 rows): Physical beds. Tracks hardware availability strictly (operational, cleaning, maintenance, out_of_service). Clinical occupancy is decoupled from physical hardware condition.

    • Group B: Human Resources & Shift Rosters

  • staff (6 rows): Healthcare staff registry with role RBAC (admin, chief_doctor, doctor, nurse, receptionist, accountant) and base salaries.
  • staff_attendance: Daily shift check-in/out auditing enforced by a composite unique constraint: UNIQUE(staff_id, work_date).

    • Group C: CRM, Hotline & Outpatient Scheduling

  • patients (5 rows): Master Patient Index (MPI) with unique medical codes (FMH-2026-XXXX), emergency contacts, allergies, chronic conditions, and anonymity flags.
  • appointments: Outpatient consultation calendar with native SQL TIME and DATE columns.
  • call_logs: Inbound/outbound call center queue with multi-channel source attribution (hotline, telegram, instagram, website).

    • Group D: Hospitalization & Nursing Vitals

  • admissions (5 rows): Inpatient hospitalization master container with stored generated duration (total_days = GREATEST(1, DATEDIFF(...))) and strict check constraints: planned_end_date >= start_date.
  • daily_logs: 24-hour nursing logs with range-validated vital signs (vital_bp_systolic 50–300, vital_bp_diastolic 30–200, vital_pulse 30–250, vital_temp 30.0–45.0, vital_spo2 50–100). Enforced by UNIQUE(admission_id, log_date).
  • bed_transfers: Ward movement audit trail capturing previous bed, destination bed, transfer timestamp, and clinical rationale.

    • Group E: Clinical EMR, Pharmacology & Epicrises

  • medical_histories: Formal clinical anamnesis (anamnesis_morbi, anamnesis_vitae, psychiatric evaluation, somatic status, ICD-10 coding).
  • medications_catalog (8 rows): Central hospital pharmacy formulary tracking stock levels, dosage forms, unit pricing, and minimum safety reserves (min_stock_level).
  • prescriptions: Clinical medication orders linked to attending doctors with administration routes, timing, and frequencies.
  • doctor_daily_notes: Attending physician progress notes tracking daily patient condition (satisfactory, moderate, severe, critical) and treatment plan adjustments.
  • discharge_epicrises: Final clinical discharge summaries detailing recovery status, final ICD-10 diagnosis, and home prescriptions.

    • Group F: Financial Ledger, Invoicing & Double-Entry Accounting

  • services_catalog (7 rows): Master clinic price list for diagnostics, procedures, consultations, and inpatient daily tariffs.
  • invoices (5 rows): Master balance container for admissions tracking total_billed, discount_amount, net_amount, total_paid, balance_due, and payment_status (unpaid, partial, paid, refund_due, refunded).
  • invoice_items: Segmented line-item charges. Single Source of Truth for all billing. Enforces CONSTRAINT chk_invoice_items_dates CHECK (service_end_date IS NULL OR service_start_date IS NULL OR service_end_date >= service_start_date).
  • payments: Signed financial receipts. Enforces CHECK(amount <> 0). Positive values represent patient payments; negative values represent patient refunds.
  • accounting_transactions: Double-entry general ledger. id VARCHAR(128) PRIMARY KEY prevents prefix buffer overflow (CONCAT('TXN-', NEW.id)). Indexed on (transaction_date, transaction_type).
  • audit_logs: System-wide data mutation tracking storing actor staff IDs, IP addresses, action types, and old_data_json/new_data_json audit diffs.

    6. Analytical Views Engine

    1. v_bed_live_status (14-Bed CAD Map Engine):

    Window ranking orders by a.start_date ASC, a.created_at DESC resolving turnover collisions. Includes next_reserved_date projection. Non-operational or available beds have patient demographics masked cleanly.

    2. v_financial_ledger (Hospital Balance Sheet):

    Computes individual patient balances and provides live breakdowns:

    * paid_cash: Sum of cash and cash_register.

    * paid_terminal: Sum of terminal.

    * paid_card_online: Sum of card_transfer and payme_click.

    3. v_patient_full_profile (360° Patient Dossier):

    Consolidates active clinical diagnoses, allergies, current ward, attending physician, net billing, outstanding debt, active prescriptions, and completed nursing logs.

    4. v_daily_hospital_kpi (Executive Dashboard Snapshot):

    Aggregates total beds, occupied beds, available beds, occupancy %, active inpatient counts, gross revenue, collected revenue, and debt receivables.

    5. v_pharmacy_low_stock (Automated Procurement Deficit Alert):

    Identifies medications where stock_quantity <= min_stock_level and computes the exact deficit quantity for supplier replenishment.

    7. Python Transactional Service Layer (`db.py`)

    Multi-table clinical workflows run inside atomic Python transactions, decoupling business workflows from database hooks:

  • admit_patient(...): Atomically creates admission, validates against overlapping active stays, initializes master invoice, inserts initial invoice_items bed segment with human-readable bed_code, and sets patient status to active.
  • transfer_patient_bed(...): Validates target bed against overlaps, handles same-day switches without tariff inflation (t_date == d_prev_start), clamps previous segment via dialect-agnostic Python string formatting, transitions departed bed to 'cleaning', logs bed_transfers audit record, and updates admissions.bed_id.
  • discharge_patient(...): Atomically sets discharge timestamp, prunes any future segments that started after a backdated discharge date, clamps the active bed segment, transitions bed hardware status to 'cleaning', triggers automatic recalculation of final stay tariffs, and flips overpaid invoices to 'refund_due'.

    • 8. The Production Trap Resolutions (v8.0 Audit Matrix)

    Trap #Root Cause IdentifiedProduction Hardening Applied
    **1****Post-Discharge Sanitation Gap:** beds.status remained &#x27;operational&#x27; immediately upon discharge, risking reassigning unsterilized beds.discharge_patient and transfer_patient_bed transition departed beds to **beds.status = &#x27;cleaning&#x27;**.
    **2****Date Inversion Crash on Backdated Discharges:** Backdated discharges caused service_end_date &lt; service_start_date on newer segments, throwing check constraint errors.discharge_patient now **prunes future segments** (DELETE WHERE service_start_date &gt; actual_end_date) before clamping active stay.
    **3****Same-Day Transfer Tariff Inflation:** Transferring beds on admission day billed 1 day on old bed + N days on new bed ($1+5=6$).If t_date == d_prev_start, the prior segment is **overwritten directly** with new bed rate and bed ID (0 nights on old bed).
    **4****Cancelled &amp; Refunded Invoices Labeled &#x27;Unpaid&#x27;:** When admissions were cancelled and refunded ($0 debt, $0 revenue), they fell through to &#x27;unpaid&#x27;.Added trigger condition **WHEN NEW.total_billed = 0.00 AND (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM payments WHERE invoice_id = NEW.id) &gt; 0 THEN &#x27;refunded&#x27;**.
    **5****Concurrent Double-Booking Race Condition:** Concurrent admissions on the same physical bed created ghost occupants.Enforced **overlap validation checks** in admit_patient and transfer_patient_bed before committing.
    **6****Missing Daily Ledger Index:** Full table scans on daily cash register closes (kassa_sverka).Added **CREATE INDEX idx_accounting_txn_date ON accounting_transactions(transaction_date, transaction_type)**.
    **7****Turnover Day Collision in CAD Map:** Patient B&#x27;s newer created_at hid Patient A on turnover morning.Tied tie-breaker to **a.start_date ASC, a.created_at DESC**. Incumbent holds bed until discharged.
    **8****Python Dialect Crash on SQLite Fallback:** CONCAT() in db.py crashed in SQLite.Replaced with **Python string formatting** before SQL execution.
